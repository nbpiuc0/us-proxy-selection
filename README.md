## What "Private Proxy" Actually Means (And What US Sellers Hope You Don't Notice)

A **private proxy** is an IP address assigned to one user at a time. No sharing, no overlaping sessions, no random stranger on the same exit node tanking your reputation score. Compare that to **shared proxies**, where dozens of users rotate through the same IPs — cheaper, but Netflix bans them in twenty seconds.

When someone says "buy US private proxy," they usually mean one of three things:

- **Dedicated datacenter proxy** — a private IP hosted in a US data center. Fast, cheap, easy to detect.
- **Static residential / ISP proxy** — a private IP that looks like a real home connection but lives in a data center. Slower than datacenter, way harder to flag.
- **Residential proxy with US targeting** — rotating real-home IPs in US states. Expensive per gigabyte, but the gold standard for stealth.

Most buyers don't realize there's a difference until they buy the wrong one and watch their scraper get blocked an hour in.

## Tips Most "Buy US Private Proxy" Guides Skip

A few things I learned the hard way:

- **Rotate User-Agents alongside proxies.** A static UA on rotating IPs is a fingerprint. Anti-bot systems notice.
- **Match proxy type to target.** Cloudflare-protected sites laugh at datacenter IPs. Use residential or ISP.
- **Stager request timing.** Even private proxies get flagged when you hit a site at machine-perfect intervals.
- **Kep authentication credentials out of git.** Obvious in theory, ignored constantly in practice.
- **Test from your actual production environment.** Proxies that work from your laptop sometimes fail from a cloud VM because the cloud IP gets blocked separately.
